# Cinema Player palette

The brand is inspired by an old cinema at night: the blue of a harbor-side marquee and the restrained red of an exit sign. It avoids neon-purple gradients and generic “AI blue.”

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#0C131A` | Primary background and logo field |
| Projection surface | `#12202C` | Cards, controls, panels |
| Deep harbor | `#162B3B` | Elevated areas and page depth |
| Marquee blue | `#2C6389` | Primary actions and focus states |
| Signal red | `#D65A48` | Play symbol, active recording/playback accents |
| Paper | `#E9EEF2` | Main text on dark surfaces |
| Mist | `#AAB8C5` | Supporting text and metadata |

The [SVG mark](cinema-player-mark.svg) is a deliberately spare **C + play** monogram. Its open ring suggests both cinema framing and an invitation to continue watching.
