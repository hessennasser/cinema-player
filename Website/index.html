01

Your library, remembered

Add files by panel, a drag onto the window, or a folder scan. Cinema Player keeps secure access, thumbnails, details, favorites, your viewing position, and a Finder or Trash shortcut on every title.

02

Play from a link

Press ⌘L or drop a link on the window to play an MP4, a MOV, or an HLS stream straight from the web. Paste a page address instead and Cinema Player reads the video that page exposes. Links sit in the library beside your files — and when one needs a provider's own player, it says so rather than failing quietly.

03

Controls when you need them

Move the mouse to reveal a precise progress bar, skips, speed, fit modes, volume, and fullscreen. Then they quietly disappear.

04

Subtitles, on your terms

Choose subtitle tracks built into a video or load a local SRT file. Adjust the timing when a release is a little early or late; captions stay visible while controls disappear.

05

One after another

Autoplay rolls into the next title. Step forward or back, loop one or the whole list, or shuffle — and switch the audio track when a file carries more than one.

06

Keys where you expect them

Cinema Player appears in Now Playing and Control Center and answers the keyboard media keys — play, pause, skip, and scrub without leaving the film.

07

Native by design

Built in SwiftUI and AVKit for macOS. No Electron shell, no account wall — and nothing leaves your Mac unless you hand it a link yourself.
